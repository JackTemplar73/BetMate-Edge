Trust zone: ~45% to ~60% predicted win probability

NRL is a two-team market — every game has one side near 40–60% implied. The model is best-calibrated in the near-even-money range (45–60% predicted), where predicted and actual win rates sit within ±2pp. Outside that window it over-estimates favourites (says 72%, they win 64%) and slightly under-estimates underdogs in the 35–45% band.

The $1.66–2.40 bracket is the only zone where flat-staking approaches breakeven (−2.2% and +1.0%). Short favourites at $1.05–1.40 lose 8.2% blind because the vig eats the already-thin edge. Underdogs $3.01+ lose 21.6%. The disciplined angle is near-even-money games where the model diverges from the market by ≥3 pts of line, sized with fractional Kelly and graded on CLV.
