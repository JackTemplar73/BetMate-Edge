Trust zone: ~10% to ~20% predicted win probability

The model is well-calibrated only between 10–20% predicted (gap ≤ ±1.5pp). Above 20% it is systematically over-confident — it says 35%, the runner wins 25%. Below 10% it is mildly under-confident, but that's the longshot zone where market favourite–longshot bias dominates and a 3pp edge evaporates after margin and variance.

So when is a longshot ever bettable? Only at the intersection of both charts: a runner whose true chance the model can estimate AND whose market price implies materially less. In this data that intersection is essentially empty — the model can't reliably rank one longshot above another (its 4th, 5th and 6th picks win at statistically indistinguishable rates), and the market has already priced the tail efficiently or worse. The disciplined edge is on favourites and short second-favourites the model also rates on top — the $2–3 zone — sized with fractional Kelly and judged on closing-line value, not last week's P&L.
