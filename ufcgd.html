Model Method

How individual fighter traits currently feed the dog-only guardrail and what needs to be learned next.

Current Geoff Coefficients

Coefficients are now dynamic per bout. They move by fighter depth, Markov history, uncertainty, opponent quality and whether recent form agrees with longer-term evidence.

Overall profileDynamic, usually largestBaseline fighter quality: skill posterior, opponent quality, rank context, striking, takedown and control profile. Gets more weight when both fighters have deeper, lower-uncertainty records.Fight pathDynamicRepeatable round pattern: control, damage, deficit risk, recovery and submission-control states. Gets more weight when both fighters have enough round-state history and the path gap is meaningful.Finishing threatDynamicAbility to turn damage states into fight-ending threat. Gets more weight when finish/damage evidence is reliable.Recent formCapped at 10%Recency-weighted win trend. Cut down when it comes against weaker opposition or conflicts with longer-term profile/fight path.

Anti-Recency-Bias Rules

Avoid prospect hypeRecent form weight is reduced when the recent-form side has weaker opponent quality.Avoid small-sample recency biasRecent form weight is reduced when either fighter has limited UFC depth.Respect long-term profileRecent form weight is reduced when it disagrees with overall fighter profile.Respect repeatable fight pathRecent form weight is reduced when it disagrees with the Markov fight path.Reward reliable traitsOverall/profile and path weights rise when both fighters have deeper, cleaner histories.
